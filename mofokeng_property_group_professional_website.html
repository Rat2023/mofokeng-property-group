Who we are

A property-focused business built on relationships.

“The right introduction can be the beginning of a valuable property conversation.”

Mofokeng Property Group is a South African property sourcing and marketing venture focused on identifying potential opportunities and connecting interested parties for further discussion.

We aim to work with property owners, authorised agents, prospective buyers, investors, developers and other professionals. Our role is to help clarify what a party is looking for, gather available information and support an appropriate introduction where there is a potential fit.

We believe professional communication, realistic expectations and independent checks are essential throughout the process.

How we work

A clear process. No pressure or promises of guaranteed outcomes.

Every lead is different. These are the usual first steps for exploring whether an opportunity and a brief may fit.

01

Understand the brief

Discuss the location, property type, purpose, budget or other key criteria.

02

Collect relevant information and confirm who is authorised to share or market it.

03

Important: A lead is not a confirmed listing, an offer of finance or a promise of investment performance. Availability, ownership, authority to market, zoning, approvals, costs and commercial viability must be independently verified. No return is guaranteed.
Frequently asked questions

Before we start a conversation.

A few useful answers for property owners, prospective buyers and potential partners.

Do you only work with listed properties?

We can discuss potential listed or privately introduced opportunities, but details and availability must be checked, and the owner or representative must authorise any marketing.

Can I approach you with a property to sell?

Yes. Share the location, property type, your relationship to the property and whether you have authority to market it. Avoid sending sensitive personal documents in an initial enquiry.

Can you guarantee an investor or buyer?

No. We can explore introductions where there may be a fit, but we cannot guarantee funding, a sale, a transaction or a specific timeline.

Do you provide legal, financial or valuation advice?

No. Use appropriately qualified independent professionals for legal, tax, financial, valuation, planning and technical advice.

What should a buyer or investor include?

Share the preferred location, property category, intended use, indicative budget or acquisition criteria and your preferred way to be contacted.

How do I start?

Use the enquiry form or contact us on WhatsApp or email. Include enough information for us to understand your enquiry, but do not send passwords, banking details or confidential financial records.
